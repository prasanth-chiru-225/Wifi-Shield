Packet Sniffing

Knowledge Base

Understanding the fundamental concepts of network security.

What is Public Wi-Fi?

Public Wi-Fi refers to free, unencrypted wireless networks commonly found in coffee shops, airports, and hotels. Because they are designed for easy access, they lack the security protocols necessary to protect connected devices from unauthorized monitoring.

Why is it Dangerous?

By default, data transmitted over open public networks is not encrypted. This means any other device on the same local network can easily intercept and read your communications, including plain text passwords, session tokens, and personal messages.

How Hackers Steal Data

Cybercriminals use automated tools on public networks to execute packet sniffing and spoofing attacks. By positioning themselves logically between your device and the router, they can secretly read and modify all unencrypted data passing through.
